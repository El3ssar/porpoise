The marks are trademarks of their owners, used only to name the service the user connects to. Keep their shapes and
colours; give them clear space (the tiles leave at least a quarter of the mark's size on every side), never stretch,
recolour (except the documented white-on-dark Box variant), outline or animate them. A `<name>-dark.svg` is used in
Dark Mode when present.
